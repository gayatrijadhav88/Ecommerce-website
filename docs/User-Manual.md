# User Manual

1. Open the application.
2. Register or login.
3. Browse products from the Products page.
4. Use filters or AI semantic search for natural queries.
5. Open product details to view similar products.
6. Add products to cart or wishlist.
7. Go to Cart and proceed to Checkout.
8. Enter shipping address and place order.
9. Track orders from Orders.
10. Use AI Assistant for shopping guidance.
